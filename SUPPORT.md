# Support Guide

## How to Get Help
- **Technical Support & Bug Reports**: support@tulingcreativehub.com
- **Sales, Demos & Custom Requirements**: sales@tulingcreativehub.com
- **Main Website**: https://tulingcreativehub.com

## What to Include in a Support Request
To help us diagnose issues quickly without back-and-forth:
1. **Organization / Instance & Module**: Which product or module is involved (e.g., Admission, SIS, HCM, Payroll, Library, Tally Connector, Gate/Fee App).
2. **Environment**: Local, staging, or production.
3. **Summary & Timeline**: What happened, when it started (with timezone), and whether it is reproducible.
4. **Evidence**: Exact error messages, request/job IDs, and steps to reproduce (omit passwords, tokens, and sensitive personal records).

## Security Issues
For suspected vulnerabilities or unauthorized access, email **support@tulingcreativehub.com** with `[SECURITY REPORT]` in the subject line rather than using public channels.
