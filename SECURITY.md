# Security Policy

## Scope
This policy covers public web properties, application services, APIs, webhooks, connectors, and official repositories maintained by **Tuling Creative Hub**.

## Reporting a Vulnerability
Please report security issues privately—**do not** open a public issue or pull request for an unpatched vulnerability.

Send your report to **support@tulingcreativehub.com** with the subject line `[SECURITY REPORT] <Module or Endpoint>`. Include:
1. Affected repository, module, URL, or endpoint.
2. Clear reproduction steps or proof of concept.
3. Potential security, data, or operational impact.
4. Any relevant timestamps, headers, or non-sensitive log excerpts.

## Response & Remediation
- **Triage & Acknowledgment**: We aim to acknowledge reports within **2 business days**.
- **Fix & Verification**: Confirmed vulnerabilities are prioritized by severity, patched, and verified before public disclosure.
- **Responsible Disclosure**: Please give us a reasonable window to investigate and deploy a fix before publishing details.
